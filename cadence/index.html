02 What I do

Find the leak. Design the fix. Ship it.

Every engagement runs the same loop, and each step stands on its own.

First I find where the money leaks: the hours your people spend re-typing, chasing, copy-pasting, and reconciling — priced, per month, in a written report.

Then I design the fix: one automation, specified down to the tools, the running costs, and what your team stops doing. A blueprint anyone competent could build from.

Then I build it, run it next to your current process until it holds, and hand it over documented. You own everything. Nothing depends on me staying.

04 Method

How an engagement runs.

No workshops, no steering committees. I work where the work happens.

  1. 01 · Week 1

    Watch the work

    I sit with the people who actually do the work and time where the hours go. Not interviews about the process — the process itself.

  2. 02 · Week 2

    Price the leaks

    Every problem gets a number: hours lost, cost per month, cost to fix. You get the list, ranked. That's the audit report.

  3. 03 · Weeks 3–4

    Design one fix

    The top leak gets a full specification: workflow, tools, running costs, failure modes, and what your team stops doing by hand.

  4. 04 · Weeks 5–12

    Build and prove

    I build it and run it in parallel with your current process. It replaces nothing until it beats the manual version on your own numbers.

  5. 05 · Handover

    Hand it over

    Documentation, training, and your own accounts on everything. The engagement is designed to end. The retainer exists only if you want it.
