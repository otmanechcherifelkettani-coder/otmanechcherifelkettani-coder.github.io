## Purpose (why this course exists)
Most AI agents never leave the demo. Students learn frameworks and prompts; nobody teaches them to prove an agent
works, ship it, and hand it over. You have done exactly that at scale, and you want the next generation of Moroccan
engineers and managers to start where you finished. **[validate: is the Morocco link personal? what do you want to say about it?]**

## Positioning
For engineering and business students who want AI skills employers can check, **Agent Ship Lab** is the
hands-on program that ends with a live agent, a measured scorecard and a Production Report, unlike courses
that end with a demo or a certificate of attendance. Taught by an operator who shipped LLM support automation at
global scale (~$15M/yr saved, ~$20M/yr incremental bookings, +20% CSAT).

## Audience (first niche)
Post-bac private schools in Morocco (first target: HESTIM). Buyer = school director / program director.
Users = bac+3 to bac+5 students in engineering, computer science and management.
What the buyer wants: employability, company partnerships, visibility, something to grade.
What the student wants: a project that gets an internship, a skill that is not already on ChatGPT.

## Personality (archetype)
Primary: Creator (builds the thing). Secondary: Sage (measures and explains why it works).
Not: Magician (AI hype), not Hero (bootcamp bravado).

## Voice
- Numbers before adjectives. "Pass rate 72% → 91%" beats "much better".
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Says what will not be taught and why.
- Plain words over jargon; terms of art (evals, traces, guardrails) are defined the first time.
- Signature lines from the site, reusable: "I don't stop at the recommendation." / "If it doesn't move the number,
  we stop." / "Build it, measure it against a baseline, hand it over working."

## Narrative (trueline)
Consultant → operator → builder. Now teacher: the same method, handed to students, with the same bar
you hold yourself to at work.
