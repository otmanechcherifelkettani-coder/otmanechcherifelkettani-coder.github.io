# Brag Plan: Shipping AI That Works (Launching AI Agents in Companies)

Source: `Launching_AI_Agents_in_Companies___Student_Workshop_Pack.docx`, a two-day student workshop pack. Full /brag workflow (Hyperframes). Landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, 25.0 s. English, the pack's language.

## What is this app?
A 12-hour, two-day workshop where students take an LLM agent (a SoukGo refund agent) from problem to production decision. They leave with a bounded prototype, a versioned evaluation set and a go/no-go memo, "not just a convincing demo".

## The angle
The pack's own lecture opener, acted out: a refund agent nails the demo ("refund processed"), then the four questions nobody asked appear on top of it. "A successful demo can still be an unsafe product." The workshop is the fix: authority outside the model, evidence before shipping.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A chat UI types "My drink was missing from SG-1001. Can you refund it?" The agent answers warmly: refund processed. A small "Demo passed ✓" badge. It looks finished, and it isn't.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Four questions stack over the chat: Right customer? Already refunded? Correct amount? Did payment actually succeed?
- The thesis line: "A successful demo can still be an unsafe product."
- The money path from the pack, lighting up node by node: Identity → Read → Eligibility → Confirm → Ledger, ending on a ledger row `SUCCEEDED · MAD 20 · 1 entry`. Headline: "A model proposes; software acts."
- What you leave with: bounded refund-agent prototype · versioned evaluation set · evidence-based go/no-go memo.

## Outro / punchline
The pack's through-line: "Let evidence—not the demo—decide whether you ship." Then: 12 hours · 2 days · no coding required.

## User flow worth showing
Customer request → agent proposes a refund → server checks identity, eligibility, confirmation → ledger says SUCCEEDED. This is the workshop's build lab (policy v1.0, synthetic fixture SG-1001, drink I-2 = MAD 20).

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: confident tech-education film; a demo that gets interrogated.
- Interpretation: few scenes, long readable holds, soft slides and blur swaps, one strong reveal; no jokes beyond the demo's false confidence.

## Duration: 25.0 s

## Privacy and grounding
- All data is the pack's synthetic fixture (SoukGo, SG-1001, MAD 20). No real people, emails or company data.
- No facilitator-only content (no hidden H-cases or answer keys): the pack says those stay restricted.
- Uber is not named on screen: the pack cites Uber's public writing, but a promo naming it could read as endorsement.
- The agent's chat reply is illustrative wording, built from the lecture opener ("apologizes warmly and says the refund is processed").

## Share copy (draft)
Anyone can demo an AI refund agent. Two days to learn whether it should ship.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse, motion-matched accents.
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (120.19 BPM), from 0 s, fade under the end card.
- Music cue guidance: preset read (`cues/...vol-1...music-cues.md`). Beat grid ≈ 3.02 + 0.5k. Strong cues used: 16.02 (money path, second node), 20.02 (takeaways, second item), 23.02 (outro detail line). Sequential reveals on the grid: questions 4.52/5.02/5.52/6.02 (fast in, held together until 8.0); nodes 15.52–17.52; takeaways 19.52/20.02/20.52 then held.
- Audio-reactive: subtle; music RMS breathes the mint glow behind the title and ledger card. No visualizer.
- SFX posture: sparse. Key ticks on the typed message, a soft drop on the agent reply, small clicks on the question chips, soft impact on the title reveal, chip-lay on each node, one bell under the final line.
- Restraint rule: no effect on every beat; nothing harsh over the readable lines.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Demo — 0.0–4.0 s
Chat window "SoukGo · refund assistant (AI)". Customer message types in; agent bubble: "So sorry about that! Your refund has been processed." A small "Demo passed ✓" badge lands.
Sequential/interaction: yes — typed message, then reply, then badge.
Audio: light key ticks, soft drop on reply. Transition: soft → chat stays, dims.

### Scene 2 — Questions — 4.0–8.0 s
The chat dims; four red-dot question chips stack on the right: Right customer? / Already refunded? / Correct amount? / Did payment actually succeed? (in on the beat, then all held ≥2 s).
Audio: one click per chip. Transition: blur swap.

### Scene 3 — Thesis — 8.0–11.5 s
"A successful demo can still be an *unsafe product*." Words arrive grey → ink; "unsafe product" in forest. Holds 2.9 s.
Transition: dip → reveal.

### Scene 4 — Reveal — 11.5–15.0 s
Mono label "STUDENT WORKSHOP · 2 DAYS"; title "Shipping AI That Works"; sub "LLM agents from problem to production." Mint glow breathes with the music.
Audio: soft impact on title. Transition: slide.

### Scene 5 — Money path — 15.0–19.0 s
"A model proposes; software acts." Five nodes light in sequence: Identity · Read order · Eligibility · Confirm · Ledger. Then a ledger row: `SUCCEEDED · MAD 20 · 1 entry`.
Audio: chip-lay per node, beat-locked node 2 at 16.02. Transition: slide.

### Scene 6 — What you leave with — 19.0–22.0 s
"You leave with" + three cards: Bounded refund-agent prototype · Versioned evaluation set · Evidence-based go/no-go memo.
Audio: card-place per card. Transition: blur swap.

### Scene 7 — Outro — 22.0–25.0 s
"Let evidence—not the demo—decide whether you ship." then "12 hours · 2 days · no coding required" and the title as a signature.
Audio: bell under the line; music fades.
