# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Shipping AI That Works

## Objective
A 25 s launch-style brag video for the student workshop "Launching AI Agents in Companies" (title on the workbook: "Shipping AI That Works").

## Output
- Composition: `brag-output/workshop-pack/composition/` · Render: `brag-output/workshop-pack/brag.mp4`
- Landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, 25.0 s

## Source material
- The uploaded .docx (README, participant workbook, lecture outline, cheat sheet). Facilitator-only content is excluded.
- Verbatim copy: "A successful demo can still be an unsafe product." · "Shipping AI That Works" · "LLM agents from problem to production" · "A model proposes; software acts." · "bounded refund-agent prototype" · "versioned evaluation set" · "evidence-based go/no-go memo" · "Let evidence—not the demo—decide whether you ship." · "12 instructional hours over two days" · "No AI or coding prerequisites" · fixture "My drink was missing from SG-1001. Can you refund it?" · ledger "SUCCEEDED" · MAD 20.
- UI to recreate: the SoukGo refund chat, and the money path Identity → read → eligibility → confirm → ledger.

## Creative direction
Tone polished; a demo that gets interrogated. See brag-plan.md for angle, hook and storyboard. Avoid generic SaaS language, abstract filler, Uber branding, hidden eval cases.

## Visual identity
Ground #f5f2eb, ink #10211b, mint #6fd39f, forest #0f6b46, sand #e8e4d9, danger #d9534f. Inter 400/700/900, IBM Plex Mono.

## Storyboard
1. demo 0–8 s (chat 0–4, questions 4–8) · 2. thesis 8–11.5 · 3. reveal 11.5–15 · 4. path 15–19 · 5. leave 19–22 · 6. outro 22–25

## Audio
- Music: assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3, fade out over the last 1.5 s.
- Cues: preset grid ≈ 3.02 + 0.5k; strong cues 16.02, 20.02, 23.02 (locked to node 2, takeaway 2, outro detail).
- Audio-reactive: subtle; RMS drives the mint glow in the reveal scene and the ledger card glow.
- SFX: chosen after animation: keypresses on typing, drop on reply, clicks on question chips, soft impact on title, chip-lay on nodes, card-slide on takeaways, bell on the outro line.
