# Shipping AI That Works: university demo film

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 1 min 37 s, English voice-over (Kokoro "Onyx"), original score.
**Look:** inspired by the Flashmotion references (Atrium, Chatly AI): near-black stage, fluted blue and amber glow, kinetic type in step with the voice, then a light "product" world for the workshop itself.
**Source:** `work/demo/` (film.html, make_vo.py, timeline.py, score.py, rec.js).

## Accuracy notes
- Every instructor figure comes from the CV. The film carries the line "Independent workshop. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Uber."
- SoukGo data on screen (order SG-1001, MAD 20 drink, tool names, policy v1.0) is the fictional case from the participant workbook.
- The "11 / 12" evaluation score and the red-team message are illustrative screens, not results.
- Assessment weights (20 / 20 / 25 / 20 / 15) are from the university edition one-pager.
- Music and sound effects are original (synthesised in `score.py`), so there is no licence to credit.
