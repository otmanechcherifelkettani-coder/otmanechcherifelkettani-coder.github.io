**Palette:** off-white #F5F1E8, cobalt #2346FF, electric orange #FF5B1F, warm yellow #FFC531, deep navy #0E1A3D.

**Sources:** content comes from the participant pack (agenda, SoukGo fictional case, tools, public practice cases, evaluation, guardrails, rollout ladder, deliverables) and the corporate edition. No hidden evaluation cases or facilitator-only material appear. Uber is not mentioned.
