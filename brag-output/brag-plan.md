# /brag plan: Shipping AI That Works

Source: the uploaded PDF brochure (4 pages, in French): a 90-min conference and a 2-day hands-on workshop on AI agents for Moroccan post-bac schools.
Mode: /brag-slim (Opus 5.5). Format: landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 21 s. Language: French, matching the brochure.

## Rubric
- **What it is:** a conference (90 min) and a workshop (2 days) that teach students to take an AI agent from demo to production.
- **Who it's for:** Bac+2 to Bac+5 students in engineering, business and management (the buyer is the school).
- **What sets it apart:** it is taught by a practitioner who runs LLM agents in 51 countries, and the question is "can this ship?", not "how do I prompt?". Students build, attack and then defend a launch decision.
- **Most impressive claim:** "Animé par un praticien qui opère des agents IA dans 51 pays".
- **Visual hook:** the brochure's own thesis, *"Une démo répond à une question. Un produit porte une responsabilité."*, acted out by a refund agent that works and then fails.
- **Real UI/flow to show:** the case study (SoukGo refund agent, FR/EN/darija), Day 1 / Day 2 tags, the one-page production-readiness grid (Valeur, Fiabilité, Sécurité, Évaluation, Opérations), and the room's vote: "on lance ou pas ?".
- **Tone:** `polished` with a `default` pulse: confident, editorial, warm.
- **Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.

## Angle (v2: sell the why)
v1 sold the format. v2 sells why the skill matters, in this order:
1. **The gap:** everyone can make an AI demo, but very few can put one in production (the ×10 refund).
2. **Why now:** 200 000 AI talents targeted by Maroc IA 2030, and AI agents are the fastest-growing AI skill in hiring.
3. **The jobs:** using AI is no longer enough; AI PM, LLM engineer and AI ops roles hire for shipping it.
4. **The program**, then **the proof** (readiness grid, the room votes "on lance").
5. **What students leave with:** a working agent, tests, a decision memo, a certificate, and a real project for interviews.
Every claim comes from the brochure.
