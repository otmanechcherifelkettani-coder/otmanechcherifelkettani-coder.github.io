# Brag Plan: Shipping AI That Works (University Edition)

Source brief: the uploaded `shipping-ai-that-works-video-prompt` file. Where the brief and /brag defaults disagree, the brief wins. The film runs 60 s, not 15 to 25 s, and it uses a voiceover.

## What is this?
A proposed two-day, hands-on workshop. University students work in teams to design, build, test and evaluate an AI agent on a fictional business case.

## The angle
A premium SaaS product launch, except the product is a workshop. The agent build itself is shown as a product demo, so deans and program directors see what students actually make, not a list of course modules.

## Hook (0 to 3 s)
AI IS CHANGING HOW THE WORLD WORKS lands word by word on a dark stage. It then lifts to a header while four live agent workflows cut past: healthcare scheduling, banking support, e-commerce returns and logistics operations.

## Key moments
- An agent is assembled from five modules (language model, instructions, tools, data, workflows) as a Theory/Practice switch flips.
- The title reveal, a two-day timeline that draws itself, and a three-line credential card with no employer named.
- A 16 s product demo on one fictional case, Veloria Travel booking changes. The IDENTIFY, DESIGN, BUILD, TEST and EVALUATE steps drive the app: opportunity cards (healthcare, banking, e-commerce, travel) → workflow canvas → instructions and tool toggles → a test run with a failure → evaluation from v1 to v2 plus human-oversight controls.
- Three deliverables arrive as polished artefacts, then three benefit lines.

## Outro
SHIPPING AI THAT WORKS. FROM LEARNING AI TO BUILDING AI. BRING THE WORKSHOP TO YOUR UNIVERSITY. Then the end card, "Shipping AI That Works", alone.

## User flow worth showing
Pick an opportunity → design the workflow → connect instructions and tools → run test scenarios → read the evaluation and set risk controls.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store, leaning polished
- Creative direction: premium SaaS launch film (in the spirit of the Flashmotion launch videos the brief references; nothing copied)
- Interpretation: calm, confident pacing, with fast moves that land and hold dead still. Bold type, glassy UI cards on a deep navy stage, fluid blur-swap transitions on one continuous ground.

## Format: landscape, 1920x1080, 30 fps
## Duration: 60 s (set by the brief)

## Visual identity
The project has no site to sample, so this identity is built for the film:
- Background #07080F with indigo, cyan and violet glows
- Accent #7C7CFF (indigo), #22D3EE (cyan); status colours #4ADE80, #FBBF24, #F87171
- Text #F5F7FF, muted #9AA3BD
- Display font: Inter 900. UI font: JetBrains Mono / Inter.

## Constraints honoured
- No academy name or logo, no employer or company name or logo (Uber included), and no contact line.
- Proposed offer only: no "last cohort", no testimonials, no attendance numbers.
- Fictional businesses only (Veloria Travel and unnamed sector examples), labelled "fictional case".
- On-screen headline text appears exactly as written, in caps. The end card is "Shipping AI That Works" alone.

## Audio direction
- Role: understated ambient-electronic bed under a warm, calm voice
- Music: original score generated for this film (`build/audio.py`) at 96 BPM in an F major family. The pad enters first, a soft pluck arpeggio from 2.5 s, and a gentle pulse from the title. Off-beat hats play in the demo only. Risers and swells lead into 18 s, 44 s and 54 s, and a resolved chord rings out on the close. Pre-ducked about 8 dB under the voice.
- Music cue guidance: strong moments at 18.0, 44.0 and 54.0 s (swell hits baked into the score). Beat grid 0.625 s from 0.
- Audio-reactive: subtle. Per-frame score RMS drives the background glow intensity. No visualiser graphics.
- SFX: light UI accents on transitions and visible actions (soft impacts on scene changes, clicks on cursor actions and tool toggles, a quiet bell on the end card).

## Storyboard
1. **The shift (0–8 s):** headline per word → lifts to header → healthcare / banking / e-commerce / logistics workflow cards whip past, about 1.2 s each. Transition: blur swap.
2. **The university opportunity (8–18 s):** DON'T JUST TEACH AI. / TEACH STUDENTS TO BUILD IT. on the left. On the right, drifting concept words, then a THEORY → PRACTICE switch, then five modules dock around an agent core one by one, and the core turns "ready".
3. **Introducing the workshop (18–28 s):** SHIPPING AI THAT WORKS title reveal, then 2 DAYS. HANDS-ON. INDUSTRY-LED. as pills, then a Day 1 / Day 2 timeline drawing in, then a credential card (three lines, no employer).
4. **What students actually do (28–44 s):** the workspace app with the stepper IDENTIFY. DESIGN. BUILD. TEST. EVALUATE. The steps run as above, ending on the human-oversight controls.
5. **Tangible outcomes (44–54 s):** NOT JUST THEORY. REAL OUTPUTS. Prototype, evaluation report and go/no-go memo cards land with the voice, then STUDENT EMPLOYABILITY. CURRICULUM INNOVATION. UNIVERSITY POSITIONING.
6. **Closing (54–60 s):** the three closing lines, then the end card "Shipping AI That Works" alone, held for 2 s.
