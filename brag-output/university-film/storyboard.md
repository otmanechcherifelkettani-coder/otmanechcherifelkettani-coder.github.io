# Shipping AI That Works: university edition (storyboard v1, for approval)

**Formats:** 60 s master plus 30 s and 15 s cutdowns, each in 16:9 and 9:16, at 30 fps.
**Look:** original, toy-like real-time 3D (three.js), mixed with product-grade 2D interfaces and editorial type. It is inspired by BUCK's approach to playful character design and colour, with no copied work.
**Protagonist:** Najma ("star"), a soft electric-blue agent with a face screen and an orange eight-point zellige star that spins while it works. The Moroccan touch stays subtle: the star motif, a faint zellige pattern in the opportunity scene, a French-speaking customer in the SoukGo console, and the end line about Moroccan universities.
**Palette:** electric blue #1F5BFF, vivid orange #FF6A2B, warm yellow #FFC83D, mint #52DCAE, off-white #FBF4E8, deep navy #11204A. Scenes alternate full-colour backgrounds, with no dark or grey consulting look.
**Type:** Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold for headlines, JetBrains Mono for UI and labels, Inter for body text.
**Sound:** upbeat electronic track at about 116 BPM, with tactile clicks, soft pops, UI confirmations and small mechanical whirs when Najma's star spins. The audio signature is a rising three-note chime on the final title.

## Accuracy notes
- Deliverables match the packs: opportunity brief, sandbox prototype (built from a supplied baseline agent), evaluation report, risk assessment and threat model, and a go/no-go recommendation presented to a mock steering committee. No certification is claimed.
- SoukGo data shown is the fictional fixture from the participant pack (SG-1001, the MAD 20 drink, policy v1.0). Quality complaints go to a human under that policy.
- Uber appears only in the instructor scene and the voiceover, with a non-endorsement line on the final frame.
