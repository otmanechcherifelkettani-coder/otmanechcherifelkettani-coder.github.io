AI is not the hard part. Shipping it is.

Building an agent is easy. Building one that works means bounded tools, trusted systems for identity and money, evals that score actions rather than answers, and a staged rollout with stop rules. That is what Cadence Academy teaches in Shipping AI That Works, a two-day practical workshop for university and corporate cohorts.

Let the model handle language. Let trusted systems enforce authority and money. Let evidence, not the demo, decide whether you ship.
