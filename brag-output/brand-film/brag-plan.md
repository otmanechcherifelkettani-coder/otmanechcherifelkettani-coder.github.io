# Cadence Academy: brand film plan (60 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps)

**Concept:** AI is not the hard part. Shipping it is.
**Arc:** Possibility → Complexity → Clarity → Confidence → Action.
**Tone:** cinematic, polished. Swiss editorial: a 12-column hairline grid, Inter Display / JetBrains Mono, black and bone white, and one signal accent (#FF4A1C). No humans, robots, brains, purple gradients or holograms.
**Sources:** the participant pack only, for anything shown on screen: the SoukGo fictional case, its five tool contracts, public dev cases D01–D12, the release bar, the rollout ladder and the signature philosophy. Nothing from the facilitator pack appears (no hidden cases, answer keys or delivery notes).
**Music:** an original synthesized score at 96 BPM. A bar is 2.5 s and every cut lands on a beat, with a hard drop to silence at 14.375 s.
