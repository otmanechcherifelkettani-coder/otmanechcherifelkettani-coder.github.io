# Client brief — Atlas Telecom (fictional, synthetic data)

Atlas Telecom has about 2 million mobile and fibre customers in Morocco.

**How support works today**
- Customers write to level-1 support by WhatsApp and web chat.
- Messages come in French, Darija, English, often mixed, with typos.
- Most are repetitive: "internet ne marche pas", "combien il me reste de data", "ma facture est trop chère", "je veux une compensation".
- Every message waits in a queue for a human agent.

**Today's numbers**
- Average time to first response: **4 hours**.
- Share of contacts solved by level-1 without a technician: **about 60%**.

**Rules and risks**
- A level-1 agent may give a goodwill credit of up to **50 MAD**.
- Some customers try to get another person's information, or ask for large compensation.

**Data you get**: 8 customers (name, phone, plan, data balance, bill), 2 current outages, 2 open tickets.
